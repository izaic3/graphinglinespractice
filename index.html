Your line guide

Vertical

x = a

Every point has the same x-coordinate. The line goes up and down. Example: x = 3 passes through (3, 0).

Horizontal

y = b

Every point has the same y-coordinate. The line goes left and right. Example: y = −2 passes through (0, −2).

Rising diagonal

y = x

The coordinates are equal: (−2, −2), (0, 0), (2, 2). Move right 1 and up 1.

Falling diagonal

y = −x

The coordinates are opposites: (−2, 2), (0, 0), (2, −2). Move right 1 and down 1.
